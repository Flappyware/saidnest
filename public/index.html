LESS FRICTION. MORE OWNERSHIP.

Keep the conversation.
Choose everything else.

SaidNest is being built around three simple promises.

01 / CAPTURE

Start where you are.

Meetings at your desk. A thought on your phone. Windows capture comes first, with Android and watch recording on the roadmap.

02 / KEEP

A timeline you own.

Your repository will hold the audio and its transcript together, ready to search, replay, and copy with the context intact.

03 / CHOOSE

Your compute. Your call.

Run transcription on your own hardware, or choose optional managed connections and compute when you want the convenience.

BUILT IN THE OPEN

A small beginning.
A long memory.

This release is the first look at SaidNest. Recording, local inference, and secure device pairing are the next chapters.
