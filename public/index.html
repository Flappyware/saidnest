Before you begin

What works today?

The preview lets you search fictional conversations, read timestamped excerpts, and copy or download their text. It records nothing and saves nothing to a server.

What comes next?

Windows microphone and system-audio capture, recoverable local storage, and transcription on your own hardware. Secure mobile sync follows a reliable local archive.

What will be paid?

The software will remain open source. Optional subscriptions will cover managed remote connections and mobile convenience; hosted transcription will be charged by usage. Prices are not set.

Can I use it offline?

Direct local access and local inference are core requirements of the planned product. The current preview contains sample text only. Follow the roadmap.
